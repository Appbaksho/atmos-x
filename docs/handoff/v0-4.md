## What works

- **S01 Welcome:** aerial photo, wordmark + language/unit pill, the two illustrative score pills (decorative, as in the design), "Get started" → S02, "I already have an account" → `/auth/login?next=/app/onboarding/language`, and **"Try the Dhaka demo"** (sets `presentation + onboarded`, then goes to Home).
- **S02 Language:** all 10 locales in their native names, with a second line from `Intl.DisplayNames` in the current UI language (computed after hydration to avoid a Node/browser mismatch). Tapping a language switches the locale route right away. °C/°F and m²/ft² toggles.
- **S03 Location:** a real MapLibre map with the scan circle; place search through `useLocation().search` (`/api/v0/geo/search`, with the static fallback list); "Use my location" (permission is asked only on tap); radius slider + presets (guests are capped at 500 m: the 1 km preset shows a lock and opens the account sheet). "Scan" runs the four-step progress while `useZones` really runs (a slow scan finishes on its own after about 14 s). It then sets `onboarded`, saves `radius_m` and replaces to Home. The step copy is honest: OSM + Open-Meteo, no NASA/Landsat.
- **S04 Home:**
  - **Hero:** place, bell with an unread badge (`useUnreadCount`), warnings banner first (`conditions.warnings`), air temperature, feels-like, heat-risk pill, BMD-threshold pill (only when `bmd_level` is set; "· based on BMD thresholds"), "x °C above/below normal", the chips (Roof & road = own-zone estimate, humidity, wind, updated) and source chips.
  - **Below the hero:** the Heat Score card (gauge, level, "Why is it hot →" S06, cause chips that expand the server sentence, and the "+ Green" what-if scaled from `what_if_green`). Then the 5-day forecast strip, the air card (`useAir`, EPA category, first advice), trees due a photo (`useTrees` + `needsPhoto`; guests get a soft CTA instead), the hottest-zones carousel (hex thumbnails drawn from the zone's H3 cells, since there are no zone photos), and the goal card (`useGoals().active`, team name via `useTeam`, next task from `usePlan`) or a "Set a cooling goal" CTA.
  - **Farmer mode** renders `<FarmHome/>` as the whole page.
- **S05 Map:** MapLibre GL + OpenFreeMap liberty (`NEXT_PUBLIC_MAP_STYLE_URL`), restyled toward navy at runtime in dark mode, with a raster OSM fallback if the style fetch fails.
  - **Overlays:** H3 hexes (heat colours, opacity 0.55, white edges), the dashed lime scan circle, a user dot, a lime outline on the rank-1 zone, and markers for the top 3 zones (a glass name pill for rank 1, score discs for the others, "≈" for near-ties).
  - **Scan animation:** hexes fade in from coolest to hottest on every fresh scan, and the circle pulses while a scan runs. Both respect reduced motion.
  - **Ranked list:** a bottom sheet on phones (top 3, "Show all N" expands it, plus a radius chip with slider and presets) and a side panel at ≥ 1024 px (two-pane).
  - **Radius:** changes rescan after a 600 ms debounce and are saved to `prefs.radius_m`.
  - **"NASA LST" layer:** the GIBS monthly MODIS LST raster. At street zoom it shows as one flat colour; it's labelled as monthly, ~1 km.
  - **Tapping:** a hex opens its zone (or the nearest zone if the hex isn't in one); a list row or marker opens S06.
  - **Attribution:** "© OpenStreetMap contributors · OpenFreeMap (· NASA GIBS)" is always visible.
  - **Farmer mode** renders `<MyFields/>`.
- **S06 Zone:**
  - **Hero:** a non-interactive map of the zone's cells, with name, distance from the focus point, "x °C vs 20-year average" (from `history`) and the score disc + level.
  - **Heat now:** air, feels-like, and roof & road (labelled as an estimate).
  - **"Why this block is hot":** `why_text` with an "AI estimate" chip and a disclaimer, plus cause chips.
  - **"+ green" what-if.**
  - **"Over the years":** bar chart of the 20 hot-season values with Day (max) / Night (min), the 20-year average line, a linear trend, a year slider and stats. It's labelled "Air temperature, hot season" and the note says it isn't satellite data.
  - **Drivers card:** contribution bar from `causes[].params.adds_c`, then expandable sealed / green / dark-roof / density rows with "estimate" tags from `drivers.estimated`, a fix line, and "Add to goal" → `/app/goal/new?zone=…&focus=…`.
  - **CTAs:** Set a cooling goal → `/app/goal/new?zone=<id>`; See projection.
  - Guests get the account sheet instead of goal links.
- **S07 Projection:**
  - **Chart:** do-nothing vs with-plan lines from `useProjection`, with the gap shaded and a dashed goal-date marker when the active goal is for this zone.
  - **Horizon switch:** first year +4 / +9 / last year (2030 / 2035 / 2040).
  - **Range tiles** for both lines.
  - **Sliders:** trees / cool roof m² / shade, debounced 400 ms; a spinner shows while it refetches and the previous data stays visible. They start from the active goal's plan quantities when the plan is for this zone, otherwise from a UI starting point of 100 trees, 1,000 m² and 2 shade structures.
  - **Assumptions** rendered with `useTServer`, plus source chips.
  - **"Start the plan"** → S08.
- **States:** skeletons while loading; `ErrorState` with retry (compact on Home and the Map sheet); an empty state on the Map when a scan finds no zones; guest-restricted paths go through `requireAccount()`; and `useScan` clamps guests to 500 m so passive reads never trip the account sheet.

## Integrator notes / things other prompts must know

1. **MapLibre worker comes from a CDN.** MapLibre v6 loads `maplibre-gl-worker.mjs` relative to `import.meta.url`, which Turbopack can't follow. `map-view.tsx` calls `setWorkerUrl('https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/maplibre-gl@<getVersion()>/dist/maplibre-gl-worker.mjs')`, and MapLibre wraps it in a same-origin blob module worker.
   - **Effect:** the map needs jsdelivr reachable (it already needs OpenFreeMap tiles online).
   - **Fix for offline / self-hosting:** V0-1 or the Integrator copies `node_modules/maplibre-gl/dist/maplibre-gl-{worker,shared}.mjs` into `public/maplibre/` (both files, same folder) and changes that one line to `/maplibre/maplibre-gl-worker.mjs`. The service worker can then cache them.
2. **Farmer slots:** `<FarmHome/>` and `<MyFields/>` are rendered as the **entire page** (as on mobile, where those tabs swap the whole screen). V0-7 must include their own `<Screen tab>`. Today's stubs are bare cards.
3. **Query params I send to V0-5's screens:**
   - `/app/goal/new?zone=<zoneId>`, optionally with `&focus=sealed|green|roofs|density` from the drivers card.
   - `/app/log-action?goal=<goalId>&type=<action_type>` from the Home "Log" button.
   - If V0-5 uses other names, adjust `home/sections.tsx` (GoalCard) and `zone/drivers-card.tsx`.
4. **Overlay layer order:** overlays go above the last non-symbol basemap layer, so they sit over roads and buildings and under labels. Inserting below the *first* symbol layer, as most examples do, hides them under liberty's buildings.
5. **Map "ready" timing:** the map counts as ready on `style.load`, not `load`. `load` waits for every tile, which delayed the camera fit for a long time.
6. **Kit additions** (all live in my folders, not in the kit):
   - `MapView` / `MapAttribution` (map-view-lazy)
   - `RadiusControl`
   - `ZoneRow` / `ZoneMarker` / `MapScore`
   - `Gauge` / `CauseChips` / `WhatIf` (home/score-card)
   - `useScan` / `useFocusPoint` / `clampRadius` (map/use-scan): the shared "scan around the focus" composition. Plan or Ask can reuse it.

## Data / server observations (for V0-2)

- `conditions.anomaly_c` was +3.4 °C at 02:15 local time while `air_c` = 27.9 and `normal_c` = 31. It looks like it compares a daily maximum with the normal, not the current reading. The UI shows it as "x above normal"; please confirm the meaning.
- `conditions.meta.sources` always includes "BMD heat-wave thresholds (not an official warning)", even when `bmd_level` is null, so Home shows that chip outside heat waves too.
- Some zone names come back as "Zone 1" (the unnamed fallback) as plain strings.
- A zone id built from a hex that isn't in any zone triggers a fresh scan on the server (more than 60 s cold). The Map avoids this by routing hex taps to the containing or nearest zone.
- `GET /api/v0/zones/bogus` returns a bare 404. The Zone screen shows the generic error with "Try again". A JSON `{code:'zone.not_found'}` would allow a better message.

## Known gaps

- bn/ur are only partly translated (the Expo copy didn't cover onboarding, the new forecast/drivers/history strings, or the level names). V0-8 fills in the rest.
- The prototype's hour switch on the Heat Score card (Now / 3 pm / Tonight / Tomorrow) was dropped: v0 has no per-hour scores. The "City avg" markers on the driver bars were also dropped: there's no city baseline in the contract.
- The Welcome page's two example pills (86 / 49° and 31 / 36°) are illustrative, as in the approved design, and are not data.
- The GIBS LST overlay is ~1 km monthly data, so at street zoom it is one flat colour. It's there for honesty and the demo toggle, not for analysis.
- Map theme swaps (light ↔ dark while the map is open) re-fetch and restyle the basemap and re-add the overlays. This was tested by loading in each theme, not by live toggling.
- Headless screenshots couldn't confirm the Welcome Unsplash photo loads (Edge headless defers lazy images). It renders on the Home hero, which uses the same source.
